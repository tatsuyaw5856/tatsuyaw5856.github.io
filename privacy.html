Privacy Policy - GNSS-Demo

Last updated: November 7, 2026

1. Purpose

GNSS-Demo is a personal research application for collecting and storing GNSS observation data using Raspberry Pi, RTKLIB, and Google Drive.

2. Google Drive Access

GNSS-Demo uses the Google Drive API through rclone to upload GNSS observation files and verify their storage. The application requests Google Drive access with the authorization of the account owner.

The requested scope permits access to all files in the authorized Google Drive account, although the intended use is GNSS observation data management.

3. Data Storage

GNSS observation files are stored locally on Raspberry Pi devices and uploaded to the Google Drive account authorized by the user.

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored locally in the rclone configuration on the Raspberry Pi.

4. Data Sharing

This application is intended for personal research. Google user data is not sold or used for advertising. Data is processed using Google Drive and rclone for the purposes described in this policy.

5. Retention and Revocation

Observation files are retained according to the account owner's storage and deletion decisions. Google Drive authorization can be revoked from the user's Google Account settings.

6. Contact

For questions about this application, visit the project repository: GNSS-Demo project.
